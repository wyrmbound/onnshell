app/src/main/res/raw/lobby_lofi.ogg, lobby_lofi_night.ogg ("Lo-fi Lobby" and its late-night version) and
lobby_shop.ogg ("Coin Jar", the Store tune) are original loops synthesized from scratch by tools/lobby/make.py and
tools/lobby/shop.py (no samples, no third-party audio, no quoted melodies). They are covered by this repository's
MIT license like the code. Regenerate with: python tools/lobby/make.py OUT_DIR, python tools/lobby/make.py OUT_DIR night,
python tools/lobby/shop.py OUT_DIR. Random details such as drum noise depend on which tracks are rendered in the same run.
